# Skyrict Trademark Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the rules for using Skyrict trademarks, logos, and brand assets. It protects the Skyrict brand while allowing community members, contributors, and partners to use marks appropriately.

| Mark | Type | Status |
|------|------|--------|
| **Skyrict** | Word mark | Registered |
| **Skyrict** logo | Design mark | Registered |
| **skyrict** (logotype) | Stylized word mark | Registered |

This list may be updated from time to time. Marks not listed here may still be protected under applicable trademark law.

## 3. Permitted Use

### 3.1 Referential Use

You may use the Skyrict word mark in plain text to refer to the Skyrict software or project without prior approval, provided:

- The use is factual and referential, not promotional
- You do not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation with Skyrict
- You do not alter or modify the mark
- You do not use the mark as part of your own product, service, or company name

**Examples of permitted use:**

- "Built on Skyrict"
- "Compatible with Skyrict"
- "Contributor to the Skyrict project"
- "Skojrikt-powered application"

### 3.2 Use of the Skyrict Logo

You may use the Skyrict logo without prior approval **only** in the following contexts:

- Linking to the official Skyrict website or repository
- Showing that your project uses or is built on Skyrict (as a "powered by" badge)
- In non-commercial blog posts, presentations, or articles about Skyrict

When using the logo:

- Do not modify the logo in any way (no color changes, no cropping, no recoloring)
- Do not use the logo at a size smaller than 80px wide
- Do not place the logo on a busy background that reduces legibility
- Always include the ® or ™ symbol as appropriate

### 3.3 Forks and Derivative Works

You may fork the Skyrict repository and distribute derivative works under the Apache 2.0 license. However:

- You may **not** use the "Skyrict" name, logo, or branding as the name of your derivative project
- You may **not** use marks that are confusingly similar to Skyrict
- You may describe your project as "a fork of Skyrict" or "based on Skyrict" in plain text
- You must remove all Skyrict branding from your derivative work's name, logo, and marketing materials

### 3.4 Educational and Media Use

Journalists, educators, and content creators may use the Skyrict name and logo in articles, tutorials, courses, and presentations for informational purposes. This use must be accurate, non-disparaging, and comply with this policy.

## 4. Prohibited Use

You may **not** use Skyrict trademarks to:

- **Imply endorsement** — Suggest that Skyrict endorses, sponsors, or is affiliated with your product, service, or organization without written permission
- **Use in a product name** — Use "Skyrict" or any confusingly similar mark as part of your own product, service, domain name, or company name
- **Alter the marks** — Modify, recolor, stretch, distort, or create derivative versions of the logo or word mark
- **Use on merchandise** — Place Skyrict marks on physical products (t-shirts, mugs, stickers, etc.) without prior written permission
- **Use in advertising** — Use Skyrict marks in paid advertising (Google Ads, social media ads, sponsored content) without prior written permission
- **Use for promotional purposes** — Use Skyrict marks in a way that creates a false sense of urgency, scarcity, or exclusivity
- **In a disparaging context** — Use Skyrict marks in connection with content that is defamatory, obscene, or otherwise objectionable
- **As part of a logo** — Use Skyrict marks as a component of your own logo or trademark

## 5. Domain Names

You may not register or use domain names that contain "skyrict" or confusing variations thereof, including but not limited to:

- skyrict.com, skyrict.io, skyrict.dev (and other TLDs)
- skyrict-powered.com
- getskyrict.com
- myskyrict.com
- skyrict-erp.com

If you operate a legitimate open-source fork, you may use a domain that clearly distinguishes your project from the official Skyrict project (e.g., "myskyrict-fork.com") — but this requires prior written approval.

## 6. Community Projects and Events

Community meetups, user groups, and conferences may use the Skyrict name and logo in their event branding provided:

- The event is clearly a community event, not an official Skyrict event
- You include a disclaimer: "This is a community event and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Skyrict."
- You submit the proposed use to **trademarks@skyrict.dev** for review at least 14 days before the event

## 7. Partner and Integration Use

If you want to use Skyrict marks in marketing materials, case studies, partner pages, or co-branded content:

- Contact **trademarks@skyrict.dev** for approval
- Provide a description of the intended use and the context
- We will respond within 10 business days

Approved partner uses will be documented in a written agreement specifying the scope, duration, and any restrictions.

## 8. Enforcement

Skyrict takes trademark protection seriously. We will:

- Monitor for unauthorized use of our marks
- Send cease-and-desist notices for infringing use
- Take legal action where necessary to protect our brand

If you become aware of unauthorized use of Skyrict trademarks, report it to **trademarks@skyrict.dev**.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the Skyrict repository and on the official website. Your continued use of Skyrict marks after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
